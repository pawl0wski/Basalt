# Shape and depth

Flat surfaces with small radii. Layers are told apart by shade and a 1 px line, never by shadow (R01.1, A01).

## Rules

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Two radii only: 4 px for controls and 8 px for containers. | R01.12, R01.5 |
| Do not use a radius larger than 8 px. `rounded-2xl` and `rounded-full` are not available. The switch track is the only pill, through `rounded-switch` (proposal). | A01, tokens.md |
| No shadows, no blur, no glow. The utilities do not exist. | R01.1, A01, tokens.md rule 2 |
| A layer above content (menu, dialog, tooltip, popover) is `surface-raised` with a `border-divider` outline. | tokens.md |
| Do not nest a card in a card. A group is one card with divided rows. | R01.5, A01 |
