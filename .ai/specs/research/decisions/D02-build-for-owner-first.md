# D02 — Basalt is built primarily for its owner; wider adoption is a bonus, not a goal

- Date, owner: 2026-10-18, brief owner (Jakub Pawłowski, session user)
- Context and the options weighed: The workshop brief lists nine target personas and market-style success metrics (retention, paying Basalt Cloud users, community activity). The owner stated that, at the moment, the app is needed only by them, that it is not being built as a large app for the whole world, and that it would be nice if others benefit, but if not, that is acceptable. No alternative (building for a defined external segment) was chosen.
- Decision and why: The owner is the primary and currently only confirmed user. Adoption by others is welcome but is not a success condition. Reason: stated by the owner; no further rationale given.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: Persona list and market metrics in the workshop brief are aspirations, not commitments. Absence of outside demand is not by itself a reason to stop. Revisit if the owner decides to pursue Basalt Cloud or active promotion, at which point outside demand needs real evidence.
- Status: active
