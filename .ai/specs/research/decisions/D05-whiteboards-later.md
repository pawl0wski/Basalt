- Context and the options weighed: D01 put whiteboards in the MVP, but their storage format is undecided (D04). The owner chose to defer them rather than wait for the format. Offline-first was offered as MVP or Later; the owner chose MVP (agent had proposed MVP). PDF was offered as Should have or Later; the owner chose Should have.
